MD-IT-009 — Onboarding & Offboarding IT Checklist

1. Purpose

This checklist coordinates the identity, hardware, application, and security
work required when a person joins, changes role, takes extended leave, or leaves
Meridian Dynamics. It is the source for requests such as “new hire starts
Monday,” “contractor needs access,” “employee transferred departments,” and
“disable access today.” Service Desk owns checklist coordination, but each
specialist team remains accountable for its own control. The checklist prevents
late access, excessive access, unreturned equipment, orphaned licenses, and
accounts that remain active after the authorized end time.

2. Scope

The checklist applies to employees and contractors who receive a Meridian
identity, managed hardware, office access-related IT services, or business
application access. It covers pre-start preparation, first-day verification,
role changes, planned departures, and urgent involuntary separations.
Human-resources decisions, payroll, benefits, and legal records are outside this
IT checklist. A manager authorizes business need but cannot ask support to share
another person’s password or bypass identity verification. Password recovery
follows MD-IT-003, MFA follows MD-IT-004, hardware fulfillment follows
MD-IT-005, licensing follows MD-IT-006, and suspected insider misuse follows
MD-IT-008.

3. Definitions

An onboarding request creates or prepares approved IT access for a person who
has not started. An offboarding request removes access and recovers assets at an
authorized time. A role change reevaluates access rather than copying all
permissions from another employee. The authoritative start time and end time
come from the approved workforce record. Least privilege means granting only
access required by the current role. A checklist owner is the Service Desk
analyst who tracks dependencies and completion; the owner does not personally
perform every task. Employees commonly write “new starter,” “new hire setup,”
“transfer access,” “termination,” “disable account,” or “collect laptop.”

4. Policy / Procedure

For onboarding, the manager submits the person’s legal display name, workforce
identifier, department, manager, work location, start date, employment type,
standard role, hardware needs, and application needs at least five business days
before the start. Service Desk validates the approved workforce record and opens
linked tasks. IT Security creates the Meridian identity and required groups.
Workplace IT prepares an encrypted managed device. Business Applications assigns
approved license seats. The employee completes identity verification, password
setup, and MFA enrollment on or after the authorized start time; credentials are
never sent to the manager.

For a role change, the manager states access to add and remove. Existing access
is reviewed; permissions are not cloned from a peer. For offboarding, the
authorized request specifies the exact disable time, asset return plan, data
owner, and application ownership transfer. IT Security disables interactive
access and active sessions at that time. Business Applications removes roles
and recovers seats. Workplace IT records returned assets and erasure. Service
Desk closes the checklist only after each linked task has evidence of completion.
An urgent involuntary separation is coordinated privately with IT Security and
the approved workforce owner; support does not reveal the action early.

5. Priority & escalation

Service Desk owns `general_inquiry` coordination for routine onboarding,
offboarding, and role-change checklists. A request submitted at least five
business days before a planned start or departure is low urgency, P4, with a
one-business-day initial response. A new starter blocked on the authorized first
day is high urgency, P2, with a thirty-minute response and escalation to the
team holding the incomplete task. An approved urgent separation with an
immediate disable time is critical urgency, P1, and owned operationally by IT
Security until sessions and access are revoked. A missed offboarding deadline or
sign-in after the authorized end time is a security incident under MD-IT-008.
MD-IT-002 controls priority when impact differs from these examples.

6. Related documents

MD-IT-002 defines P1–P4 impact and urgency. MD-IT-003 defines first-sign-in and
assisted password recovery controls. MD-IT-004 defines required MFA enrollment.
MD-IT-005 governs device fulfillment, loaners, returns, and erasure. MD-IT-006
governs application approval and license recovery. MD-IT-008 governs unexpected
post-departure access or suspected insider misuse. MD-IT-010 applies to remote
starter equipment and work locations.

7. Revision history

Version 1.0 became effective on 2026-07-01. It establishes the five-business-day
lead time, authoritative start and end times, no credential sharing with
managers, role-change access review, linked-task evidence, and immediate
security escalation for missed revocation or post-departure access.
