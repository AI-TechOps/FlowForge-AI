# MD-IT-NNN — Document Title

## 1. Purpose

State what the document governs and the employee outcome it is intended to
produce. Keep the section self-contained and use the ticket vocabulary an
employee would use when asking for help.

## 2. Scope

Name the people, locations, devices, and services covered. State exclusions
explicitly and route excluded issues to the appropriate Meridian Dynamics team.

## 3. Definitions

Define internal service names, support terms, and common employee phrasings.
Every definition must be understandable without relying on a table or image.

## 4. Policy / Procedure

State the rules or ordered steps. Include concrete evidence requirements,
thresholds, approvals, and safe stop conditions in prose.

## 5. Priority & escalation

Name the owning team, response target, priority, impact or urgency threshold,
and escalation trigger. Cross-reference MD-IT-002 when its matrix controls.

## 6. Related documents

List related documents by resolvable `MD-IT-NNN` identifier and explain why each
one is relevant.

## 7. Revision history

Record version, effective date, and a plain-language summary of the change.
